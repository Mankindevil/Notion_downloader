# Magazine Catalog — CN / JP / Western

Real publications, their actual cover language, and how to match them. Use when the user references a specific magazine ("make it like Vogue", "ViVi style", "时尚芭莎 cover").

## Western — High Fashion

### Vogue
- **Masthead:** Custom Didone — closest free analog is **Bodoni Moda Black 900**, all-caps, ≈260px on a 1240px wide cover, letter-spacing 0.01em.
- **Palette:** White ink dominant. Single accent line in muted gold or warm red.
- **Cover lines:** Sparse — usually 2–4 short hero phrases, often single-word headlines like "POWER", "BLOOM", "NEW HEROES". Bodoni italic for hero, Marcellus SC for kickers.
- **Layout:** Masthead centered. Cover lines stacked tightly on one side (often right). No barcode visible at most issue front (it's on the back).
- **Voice:** Authoritative, restrained, slightly cryptic. "The September Issue", "The Cover Star", "Fashion's New Power".
- **Tic:** A small "·" or thin rule between sub-elements. Strong negative space.

### Harper's Bazaar
- **Masthead:** Custom Didone (Didot-based). **Bodoni Moda Black 900** or **Playfair Display Black** as analog. Slightly narrower than Vogue.
- **Palette:** White or black ink depending on the season cover. Accent often a single deep red.
- **Cover lines:** More than Vogue, but still curated. Bazaar loves italic Bodoni hero lines.
- **Layout:** Masthead centered with a date+issue rule line directly beneath ("MARCH · 2025 · NO. 3582").
- **Voice:** Confident, fashion-history-aware. "The Power List", "Style Stars", "Mode Manifesto".

### Numéro / Numéro Homme
- **Masthead:** Custom geometric serif (numero N has flat-top stress). **Bodoni Moda 700** non-italic, weight 700, all-caps as analog.
- **Palette:** Often single-color overlay (red, electric blue) across the image. Black or white ink. Numéro Homme tends toward red+black.
- **Cover lines:** Often a single hero phrase — "ÉROS", "POUVOIR", "NOIR" — large, ASCII-bold, lowercase or all-caps.
- **Layout:** Off-axis. Masthead sometimes runs vertically up the left edge. Image cropped tight.
- **Voice:** Art-magazine deadpan. Single concept per cover.

### i-D
- **Masthead:** The famous lowercase "i-D" wink — a single-glyph signature in the top-left. Use **Anton Black** or condensed geometric sans, lowercase, in a red block.
- **Palette:** White ink + one bright accent (red `#ff3a2d`, neon green, electric blue).
- **Cover lines:** Minimal. Often lowercase italic — "the youth issue", "the joy issue". Anton small caps kickers.
- **Layout:** Image often cropped to a portrait or close-up — i-D famously has subjects with one eye winked (mirroring the masthead).
- **Voice:** Punk-editorial. Single-word thematic issues. Lowercase.

### Dazed / Dazed & Confused
- **Masthead:** Custom condensed sans, all-caps tightly tracked. **Anton** or **Oswald Black 900** as analog.
- **Palette:** Anything goes — Dazed plays with color blocks, photo overlays, hand-drawn elements.
- **Cover lines:** Conversational, gen-Z casual. "the friend issue", "growing up online".
- **Layout:** Often image-on-color-block — subject's photo placed against a solid color rectangle, masthead crossing the block edge.
- **Voice:** Youth-culture, ironic, sometimes earnest.

### W Magazine
- **Masthead:** Single italic "W" — like a logo more than a wordmark. **Playfair Display Italic Black** as analog. Often huge — fills the top quarter.
- **Palette:** Editorial — white, occasional warm gold or red accent.
- **Cover lines:** Italic Cormorant Garamond hero, very minimal supporting text.
- **Voice:** Slightly art-magazine inflected, more conceptual than Vogue.

## Chinese — Fashion / Lifestyle

### 时尚芭莎 (Harper's Bazaar China)
- **Masthead:** Bodoni-derived Western masthead PLUS CJK subtitle "时尚芭莎" in **Noto Serif SC weight 900**.
- **Palette:** White ink primarily. Gold accent common for cover-star anniversaries.
- **Cover lines:** Bilingual — main hero in Chinese, kickers and dates in English.
  - *"封面故事 / COVER STORY"*
  - *"独家专访 · 春日风尚"* (Exclusive interview · Spring fashion)
- **Voice:** Glossier than Western Bazaar, more cover lines, more celebrity-focused.

### 嘉人 (Marie Claire China)
- **Masthead:** Custom condensed serif, often paired with kanji "嘉人" in heavy weight nearby.
- **Palette:** Hot pink, deep red, occasional gold. Less restrained than Bazaar China.
- **Cover lines:** Dense — 5–7 cover lines often. Bilingual.
- **Voice:** Trend-forward, more aggressive than Bazaar.

### 时尚 COSMO (Cosmopolitan China)
- **Masthead:** Custom display sans + "时尚 COSMO" hybrid English+CJK. Heavy condensed.
- **Palette:** Hot magenta, fluorescent yellow, electric blue. Loud.
- **Cover lines:** Very dense — 7–10 cover lines, mixed colors, italics, hearts.
- **Voice:** Pop, dating-advice-coded, conversational. The Asian Cosmo voice.

## Japanese — Fashion / Lifestyle

### ViVi
- **Masthead:** Italic high-contrast serif **Playfair Display Black Italic** or custom, with thick paper-shadow (white + gold echo). Always italic, always heavy.
- **Palette:** Hot pink, baby blue, gold, cream paper. Pastel-pop.
- **Cover lines:** 4–6 mid-size cover lines in *different colors per line* — pink, teal, mustard. Mix of katakana headlines and English.
  - *"今月のモテ服!"* (this month's flattering outfits)
  - *"夏の新色LIP"* (summer's new LIP colors)
  - *"ViViモデル特集"* (ViVi model feature)
- **Voice:** Conversational, advice-format ("how to", "the new", "this month"). Lots of "!".
- **Tic:** Hearts, stars, sparkles scattered. Round price badge tilted -8°. The "今月の___" template phrase is very Vivi.

### CanCam
- **Masthead:** Italic script "CanCam" in pink/red. Italianno-like.
- **Palette:** Pink-cream-gold. Always feminine.
- **Cover lines:** Soft advice-format. "モテる" themes frequent.
- **Voice:** Trad-feminine office-lady fashion. Slightly older skew than ViVi.

### anan
- **Masthead:** Custom lowercase "anan" — geometric, three-letter, slightly playful. **Inter Black 900** lowercase as analog.
- **Palette:** Often single-color photo cover with white ink (anan does themed issues — sex issue, body issue, etc.).
- **Cover lines:** Minimal. "Sex Issue", "Health & Beauty Issue".
- **Voice:** Health/sexuality/wellness inflected.

### JJ / Mina / Ray
- Mid-tier glossy Asian fashion — similar to ViVi but with subtle variants in color and target demo. JJ uses more cream/beige. Mina uses more muted pastels. Ray uses bolder pink+navy.

### Newtype
- **Masthead:** Custom display "Newtype" with stylized "N". Heavy geometric sans.
- **Palette:** Often black background with brand-color accent matching whatever anime is on the cover.
- **Cover lines:** Anime episode/character feature names. Bilingual JP+EN.
  - *"特集 [Anime Title] 最新情報"*
- **Voice:** Magazine-of-record for anime. Authoritative.

### Megami Magazine
- **Masthead:** Italic script "Megami" — Italianno-like.
- **Palette:** Pink, blue, varies by featured anime.
- **Cover lines:** Anime feature headlines, often single-character close-ups.
- **Voice:** Anime character idol-magazine.

## How to use this catalog

When the user says "Vogue-style", "ViVi style", "时尚芭莎 cover", look up the entry and:

1. Pick the **masthead font + size** from the entry.
2. Adopt the **palette** as your starting CSS variables.
3. Match the **cover-line voice** — even if the subject is from an anime, write the cover lines in the publication's voice (ViVi's "今月の___" template, Vogue's single-word hero, i-D's lowercase deadpan).
4. Apply the **layout tic** (Vivi's tilted badge, Numéro's color overlay, i-D's red block masthead).

If the user names a publication you don't have here, ask them to share a reference image so you can extract its design DNA.
